Quick Answers

Common Questions

Stuff people usually text Nick about the week before the tournament.

Who can play?

Family members and invited guests who want a fun, competitive two-day event at Diamond Hills. Open to adults of all skill levels. As long as you keep pace, post an honest score, and can handle some friendly trash talk, you're in the right place.

What skill level?

All skill levels. We use handicaps and average scores to balance the teams and pairings so nobody gets steamrolled. If you don't have an official handicap, give us your honest average 18-hole score.

How does scoring work?

Each match is worth 1 point for your team. Halved matches award 0.5 points to each side. We use net scores where it makes sense so handicaps still matter. Team with the most total points wins.

Handicaps & averages

Use your official GHIN index if you have one. Otherwise give us your honest typical 18-hole score at Diamond Hills or a similar course. Outliers will be smoothed out so teams stay balanced.

Rain & weather

We play through normal Arkansas spring weather. If there's lightning or the course closes, we'll adjust tee times or format on the fly and communicate through the family group chat.

Guests & non-golfers

Non-golfing family is welcome to hang out, ride along when carts allow, and join for lunch and photos. The $150 entry covers registered players only — spectators are always free.
